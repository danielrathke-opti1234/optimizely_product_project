Executive Summary

  • Across 27 evaluated experiments reaching an estimated 31.5M visitors, the program is modeled to have generated approximately $56.4M in theoretical positive value, or $14.1M after applying a conservative 25% rollout assumption.
  • Experimentation also acted as a risk shield: roughly $19.9M of value was protected by identifying losing and negative-impact variations before they could be rolled out to all passengers.
  • Value is concentrated in a small number of high-reach winners — the top 3 experiments alone account for the majority of stand-alone modeled upside, led by the Homepage credit-card pre-qualification creative.
  • Most seasonal banner, countdown-timer, and page-visit guardrail tests showed near-zero measured lift, indicating the true value engine is a focused set of conversion-oriented redesigns (Seats, Bundles, Hotels, Credit Card).

Metric & Funnel Insight

Where value is created

Because scope is All Experiments, value is aggregated across each experiment's primary metric. The most influential contributions come from conversion-focused ancillary revenue tests — Extra Seat add-to-cart, Bundles continuation, and Hotels conversion — plus the high-traffic Credit Card pre-qualification creative on the homepage.

Where lift is flat

Seasonal sale banners, countdown timers, and page-visit guardrail tests consistently registered ~0% measured lift. These behave as protective / instrumentation experiments and contribute little to modeled upside — a signal to reallocate design effort toward conversion redesigns.

Methodology & Calculation Notes

The Portfolio Value Model was selected because Revenue Per Passenger is a reach-based unit. Modeled value scales with the number of visitors exposed to experimentation, the per-visitor value, and the visitor-weighted lift across the portfolio.

Theoretical Positive Gain = Effective Program Reach × Revenue Per Passenger × Positive Portfolio Lift Multiplier
Theoretical Loss Avoided = Effective Program Reach × Revenue Per Passenger × Weighted Negative Signal Multiplier
  • Reach de-duplication: total visitors are counted as MAX(total_visitors) per unique experiment, then summed across all modeled experiments — never summed across result rows.
  • Lift normalization: percentage lifts are converted to decimals before arithmetic (e.g., 3.0% → 0.03).
  • Impact classification: positive vs. negative impact uses each result's is_positive signal (direction-aware), not the raw sign of lift.
  • Multipliers: Positive Portfolio Lift Multiplier ≈ 1.50% (visitor-weighted across positive rows); Weighted Negative Signal Multiplier ≈ 0.53% (visitor-weighted absolute lift across negative rows).
  • Rollout: the 25% Conservative Rollout Factor is applied to Positive Gain only; Loss Avoided is retained in full by default.
