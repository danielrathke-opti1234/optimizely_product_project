Why This Campaign Was Selected

1. Proven Creative

The "Ancillaries" variation of Test-FlightSelect/My trips/Seat-Ancillaries drove a statistically significant +171% lift in Click_Checked across 1.1M visitors. The mechanism to move baggage attachment is already validated on this exact page.

2. Behavioral Audiences Ready

ODP-powered behavioral segments already exist — Searched Flights to CUN (>1 in last 30), Searched for a Flight >1 in last 30, and Customer Booking with a Domestic Flight — so no new audience engineering is required.

3. Risk Containment

The global test showed a -2% confirmation drag at 100% traffic. Restricting the experience to repeat, high-intent leisure searchers captures the ancillary lift while shielding low-intent visitors from added friction.

4. Measurement In Place

The Click_Checked and AddBaggage events plus the Confirmation [NGEN] guardrail are all live, so impact can be measured from day one with no tracking work.

Activation Method Guidance

Why rules-based (behavioral) to start? With zero live personalization campaigns, the fastest path to value is deterministic behavioral targeting: the audiences already exist, the winning creative already exists, and the metrics already fire. There is no learning ramp-up and no engineering dependency — you can be live in days.

Where a Contextual Bandit fits later: Once the behavioral baggage and insurance campaigns are producing steady attachment, the Flight Select combo/ancillary slot is a strong bandit candidate (multiple proven presentations + one clear metric + very high decision volume). Treat that as phase two, after the rules-based wins bank early value.
