Metric Focus Insight

What this metric represents. Home Page BoA Overlay Continue Rate measures the share of visitors who, after seeing the Bank of America credit-card promotional overlay on the homepage, click through to continue — a top-of-funnel engagement signal that feeds downstream booking and ancillary revenue.

What the results mean. Both production experiences redesigning this overlay drove statistically significant increases in continue rate: the mobile redesign lifted continue rate by roughly 179% (from ~6.8% to ~18.9%) and the desktop redesign by roughly 32% (from ~6.1% to ~8.0%). The mobile experience is the dominant value driver in this metric focus. Because a Metric Focus was selected, all modeling above is scoped to this metric only and has not been substituted with any other metric.

Methodology & Calculation Notes

Model selected: Portfolio Value Model. The business input is Revenue Per Passenger ($119), a reach-based unit, so program value is modeled across evaluated visitor reach rather than discrete conversion events.

Positive Portfolio Lift Multiplier = visitor-weighted avg normalized lift (positive rows) = 110.65%
Weighted Negative Signal Multiplier = visitor-weighted avg |normalized lift| (negative rows) = 33.00%

Theoretical Positive Gain = Effective Reach × Value × Positive Lift Multiplier
  = 162,176 × $119 × 1.1065 = $21,355,185
Realized Positive Gain = Theoretical Positive Gain × Rollout (1.00) = $21,355,185

Theoretical Loss Avoided = Effective Reach × Value × Negative Signal Multiplier
  = 162,176 × $119 × 0.33 = $6,368,652
Realized Loss Avoided = Theoretical Loss Avoided (rollout not applied) = $6,368,652

Reach de-duplication. total_visitors repeats on every result row for an experiment, so reach is computed as MAX(total_visitors) per unique layer_experiment_id, then summed across the 3 modeled experiments.

Normalization & direction. Percentage lifts are normalized to decimals before arithmetic, and positive vs. negative business impact is determined by the is_positive flag (not the raw sign of lift). The Conservative Rollout Factor is applied to Positive Gain only; Loss Avoided is not discounted by default.
