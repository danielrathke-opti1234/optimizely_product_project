Context for Pricing Decisions

  • Frequency is customer-configurable. Credits scale directly with how often a customer schedules each primitive. Pricing should assume a cadence, not a fixed weekly draw.
  • The multi-experience upgrade raised per-build value. P3 now produces 3 distinct audiences, 3 experiences, and 3 tailored custom-code blocks — genuine 1:many personalization, not a single headline swap. This added roughly +75 credits per Build run versus the earlier single-experience version.
  • Training runs inflate the historical average. Several high-credit runs occurred during active pipeline development and debugging. Steady-state runs trend toward the lower end of each range.
  • Recommended budgeting figure: use ~1,200 credits per campaign build as the unit of pricing, then multiply by the customer's expected campaigns-per-month.
