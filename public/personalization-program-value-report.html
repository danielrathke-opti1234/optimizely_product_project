Executive Summary

  • Tier A (headline): 4 campaigns delivered an Observed Positive Gain of $18,434.84 (≈84.4 incremental leads at $218.32 per lead). After the 25% Conservative Rollout Factor, Realized Positive Gain is $4,608.71.
  • Downside Detected: 5 campaigns produced fewer leads than their holdbacks, totaling $75,525.67 (≈345.9 fewer leads). Most of it came from [250603 Felix CMAB] CMS hero ($44,321.40). This is shown separately and is not netted against gains.
  • Cross-check: When all 9 modeled campaigns are pooled together, the combined lift is −5.12%, a Pooled Portfolio Estimate of −$81,481.24. That gives a Realization Ratio of −174.11% against the $46,797.70 Sum of Individual Wins. Taken together, the evidence points to net downside, so the positive results should be treated as unconfirmed.
  • Coverage: 9 of 19 eligible campaigns were financially modeled. 8 had no holdback (0%), and 2 tracked no lead or form-submission metric. None of the lead results was statistically significant at 95%. Three positive campaigns rest on holdbacks of 17–193 sessions with a single holdback lead, so their large lifts are fragile.
  • Supporting signals: On three CMAB hero campaigns, hero CTA click metrics fell sharply (−96% to −99%), and one of those drops is significant. We recommend reviewing event tracking on the personalized hero variants.

Methodology, Caveats & Reconciliation

Collapse methodology
  • Scope: Only Optimizely Personalization campaigns with status Paused, Concluded, or Archived are included, and only if their activity window overlaps 2025-10-01 to 2026-10-14. Running, Not Started, and other statuses are excluded. Campaign names containing "(QA Cookie)", "SP Test", or "Temporary Hide" are also excluded (none matched). Campaign and experience types were confirmed as Personalization from platform metadata.
  • One calculation for every campaign and every tier: Personalized Sessions × Relative Lift vs. Holdback × Value per Session.
  • Business Value basis: "Dollar Per Lead Conversion" at $218.32 is a value per outcome, specifically a lead. Value per Session = holdback lead rate × $218.32, which equals incremental leads × $218.32. This single basis applies to every campaign. For revenue-per-visitor or revenue-per-session bases, the entered amount would be used directly as the value of a personalized session. RPV is treated as revenue per personalized session because Personalization measures reach and results by session.
  • Matching outcome only: The lead value applies only to lead or form-submission conversions. In this project that is Form success, plus Get Started Form Submit where it recorded results. CTA clicks, navigation clicks, scroll, and bounce metrics are not leads and are never monetized.
  • One metric, one value per campaign: All primary and secondary metrics were evaluated. Each campaign uses the first lead metric that has results (primary first, then lowest metric order) and contributes one value. Other metrics appear as supporting signals only.
  • Campaign-level aggregation: Each campaign is valued from Optimizely's campaign summary, which combines all experiences and compares them with the campaign holdback. Experience rates are never averaged, and experience lifts are never summed.
  • Three tiers: Tier A is the headline value. Tier B is the upper bound from summing individual winning experiences against their own holdbacks. Tier C pools all comparable campaigns, including wins, losses, and neutral results, to check consistency. Summing only wins tends to overstate combined impact because favorable noise is more likely to look like a win. Tier C and the Realization Ratio show how much that may apply here. Tier C requires at least 5 comparable campaigns and applies the pooled lift only to each campaign's own sessions. The Realization Ratio is a consistency diagnostic, not a confidence score.
  • Observed period, not annualized: Values reflect each campaign's measured results window as reported by Optimizely. Campaign results cover the full campaign lifetime and cannot be sliced by date. Five modeled campaigns started before 2025-10-01, and Opticon geo's window ended on 2025-10-01. Nothing is projected to 365 days.
  • Conservative Rollout Factor (25%, user provided): This applies only to Tier A Positive Gain. Downside Detected is reported at full value, and Tiers B and C are not reduced. The factor is a realization assumption, not statistical confidence.
  • Statistical caveats: No lead result reached significance at 95%. Several holdbacks are very small (17–193 sessions) and contain a single lead, so large lifts in those campaigns are highly uncertain.
  • Modeled estimates, not ROI: Costs are not included. Campaign audiences may overlap, so summed campaign sessions are not unique program reach.
  • Data sources: Optimizely Personalization campaign and experience results, plus campaign metadata, for project 26942930964. Personalization campaigns are not part of the A/B Program Reporting dataset.
