📋 Executive Summary

Of the 492 accounts paying for Optimizely's Personalization product, 304 accounts (61.8%) have not created a single personalization campaign in the last 6 months, while only 188 (38.2%) have. This represents a significant adoption gap and a potential churn risk on a substantial portion of P13N ARR. This report outlines the scope, possible root causes, and recommended actions.

🔍 Possible Root Causes

Why 304 accounts are paying for Personalization but haven't started a campaign

👥

Lack of Resources

  • • Teams may not have a dedicated personalization practitioner or CRO specialist to build and manage campaigns
  • • Technical setup (audience creation, targeting rules, variation development) requires expertise many teams lack
  • • Smaller accounts may have purchased P13N as part of a bundle but don't have bandwidth to operationalize it

💡 Signal: Check if these accounts also show low MAU across all Optimizely products

🎯

Higher Priorities

  • • Teams may be focused on A/B testing velocity first and see personalization as a "phase 2" initiative
  • • Organizational pressure on other metrics (conversion rate, revenue optimization) deprioritizes P13N
  • • Migration, replatforming, or internal restructuring may have paused experimentation programs

💡 Signal: Cross-reference with EXP activity — are they running A/B tests but not P13N?

💡

Lack of Strategy / Ideas

  • • Teams don't know what to personalize — they lack a hypothesis framework for personalization use cases
  • • No clear playbook for audience segmentation strategies (behavioral, demographic, contextual)
  • • Uncertainty about ROI expectations — teams don't understand the incremental lift personalization delivers vs. A/B testing

💡 Action: Offer personalization playbooks, industry-specific use case libraries

📢

Lack of Awareness of the Feature

  • • Users may not realize they have access to Personalization features in their current plan
  • • The feature may not be prominently surfaced in the product UI — discoverability gap
  • • Stakeholders who signed the contract may not be the practitioners who use the platform daily

💡 Action: In-product nudges, onboarding flows, and CSM-led awareness campaigns

📐 Methodology & Account Reconciliation

The adoption denominator is the 492 accounts on the Personalization ARR list. Within that population, 188 (38.2%) created ≥1 P13N campaign in L6M and 304 (61.8%) did not — these tie out exactly (188 + 304 = 492).

Note on the discrepancy: A raw count of all accounts that created a P13N campaign in L6M returns 230, not 188. The difference is 42 accounts that ran personalization campaigns but do not appear on the Personalization ARR list (e.g., Zoom, Capital One, Equifax, Morningstar, University of Melbourne, Crocs). These are most likely Feature Experimentation customers running p13n-typed rules/flags, accounts with bundled or trial access, or timing mismatches between the point-in-time Finance ARR snapshot and the L6M event window. Because they are outside the paying-Personalization population, they are excluded from the adoption denominator and do not inflate the 304 "Campaign Not Started" figure.
