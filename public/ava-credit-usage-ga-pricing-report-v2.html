Context for Pricing Decisions

  • Price the unit, not the week. Credits scale directly with configured cadence. Recommend anchoring on ~1,200 credits per campaign build plus ~280 credits per monthly program report, then multiplying by expected customer usage.
  • The Monthly Report is cadence-independent. At one run per month it contributes a flat ~3,360 credits/year no matter how often campaigns are built, making it easy to bundle into a base subscription tier.
  • Multi-experience builds raised per-campaign value. The Build job now produces 3 distinct audiences, 3 experiences and 3 tailored custom-code blocks — genuine 1:many personalization rather than a single headline swap. This added roughly 75 credits per Build run over the earlier single-experience version.
  • Validation runs inflate the historical average. Several high-credit runs occurred during active pipeline development and debugging. Steady-state production runs trend toward the lower end of each range.
