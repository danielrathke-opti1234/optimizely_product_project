The Personalization Strategist finds where a personalized experience would change an outcome, designs and builds the campaign in Optimizely, watches how it performs, and tells the customer what to do next. It is the only VT in this generation with a real Act tier — it creates audiences, campaigns, and experiences directly in Optimizely, staged and human-gated before any activation.

Two Operating Lanes

The Personalization Strategist works in two distinct lanes that share the campaign ledger, the monitoring job, and the intelligence job — but have separate entry points, separate agents, and separate handoff contracts.

OPP

Opportunity-driven lane

The VT finds the opportunity from data. Signal Collector pulls GA4, Optimizely Analytics, and prior Experimentation history into a unified snapshot. The Opportunity Analyzer ranks evidence-based opportunities. Design → Build follow in sequence, each passing a deterministic handoff contract to the next step. Customer approves before anything goes live.

ASS

Customer-idea lane (assisted)

The customer brings the idea. The Campaign Copilot walks them through it conversationally — page, audience, experiences, targeting, measurement — validates each input against the live project and ledger for per-campaign deduplication, then writes two handoff files and fires the Build Assisted Campaign workflow via run_job. The workflow runs Assisted Campaign Designer → Assisted Campaign Builder → Assisted Build Composer. Copy is built exactly as provided; unsubstantiated claims are flagged before activation.

What both lanes share

Shared

Both lanes write to the same ledger. Every built campaign gets a row with its campaign_id, target page, treatment intent, status, and build date. The Signal Collector reads it before every Identify run for de-duplication and backpressure.

Written by
Campaign Builder (opportunity lane) · Assisted Campaign Builder (customer lane)
Backpressure

Monitor Campaign Performance

Shared

Personalization Impact Overview

Shared

Strategic Program Intelligence Hub analyzes portfolio health, coverage, patterns, feature adoption, and maturity across all campaigns from both lanes, delivering next-action recommendations.

Activation guardrail — applies to both lanes

Customer approval required before any campaign launch or production change

Written into AGENTS.md Operating Rules, the derived config file, the Campaign Builder prompt, and the Assisted Campaign Builder prompt. Campaigns are created as paused. No agent can activate or modify a live campaign. Design and build may proceed without approval; activation may not.

Extract tier contract

External sources are Extract-only

Only Extract-tier agents query GA4, Optimizely Analytics, or the Experimentation API. Enrich, Act, and Composer agents work from disk — making every output reproducible.

OA awareness is now wired

As of v1.1.0/v1.2.0 (2026-08-24), both collectors read oa_analytics_configured. false eliminates unnecessary query attempts. true surfaces a specific permissions message rather than a generic failure.

Ledger lives at Extract tier

The campaign ledger read and backpressure check happen inside the Signal Collector — before any analysis. De-duplication and gate logic are informed by verified platform state, not just stored ledger values.

Act Agents

Tier 5 — the only agents that write to the Optimizely Personalization platform. Two Act agents, one per lane. Both create audiences, campaigns, and experiences via the Experimentation API, stage everything as paused, and require explicit customer approval before anything can go live. This tier is absent from every other VT in this generation.

What makes this tier significant

Most VTs in this generation stop at the Composer tier — they report and recommend but do not act. The Personalization Strategist creates real Optimizely entities: audience conditions, campaign configuration, experience variations, and goal definitions. The customer moves from insight to a staged, review-ready campaign without leaving the VT conversation.

Design note: assisted-lane backpressure is informational, not a gate

The Copilot does not enforce the global staged_campaign_backpressure_threshold — and that is intentional. The threshold was designed for autonomous pipeline activity, not customer-initiated builds. Blocking a user who explicitly asked to build a campaign because the automated lane queued other campaigns would be confusing and wrong. Post-GA: surface an informational count of staged campaigns at conversation start without blocking.

Key file rules

AGENTS.md is the single source of truth

The derived files carry a DERIVED FILE header. When the two disagree, AGENTS.md wins and the derived file is regenerated. Memory files must never restate AGENTS.md config fields.

Campaign ledger is shared state

The ledger is the one file both lanes write to. It is also what the Signal Collector reads before every Opportunity Board run, making it the effective gate between past campaigns and future recommendations.

Working files are ephemeral

Extract and enrich working files are overwritten on each run. Durability comes from the dated archives in jobs/*/. The ledger is the only per-campaign durable record outside of archives.

Post-GA Enhancement

1

Campaign Copilot: add informational backpressure awareness

Post-GA

The Signal Collector enforces the global staged_campaign_backpressure_threshold before producing an Opportunity Board. The Copilot reads the ledger for per-campaign deduplication but does not check the total staged-campaign count against the threshold. This is intentional design — customer-initiated builds should not be blocked by autonomous pipeline activity. A customer who has several unactivated staged campaigns might simply not realize they exist when starting an assisted build.

Post-GA EnhancementAt the Copilot’s first turn, surface a brief informational note when staged campaigns exist: “You have N campaigns staged and waiting for activation in Optimizely.” Do not block the build — let the customer decide. This is awareness, not a gate.

Enrich

The Campaign Design Composer emits a machine-readable build handoff alongside the customer-facing canvas. The Build Handoff Reader validates it before the Campaign Builder touches the API. Nothing is inferred across the Design → Build boundary.

Why it mattersA deterministic contract means Design failures produce a validation error, not a malformed campaign in Optimizely. The boundary is explicit, auditable, and testable.
3

Two-lane architecture with shared campaign ledger

Ledger

Both lanes write to the same ledger. The Signal Collector reads the full ledger before every Opportunity Board run, so de-duplication and backpressure work across both lanes. A customer who builds an assisted campaign Monday will not see the same opportunity surface Friday.

Why it mattersThe ledger is the single source of campaign truth across the full program — not siloed by which lane created the campaign.
4

Backpressure control

Ledger

The Signal Collector enforces staged_campaign_backpressure_threshold: 5. When five or more campaigns are staged and awaiting activation, the Analyzer suppresses Priority 1 recommendations. The Backpressure Notice tells the customer exactly how many staged slots are in use.

Why it mattersWithout backpressure, a VT could produce an unlimited queue of staged campaigns the customer never activates. Backpressure prevents the pipeline from outrunning the customer's review capacity.
5

Claims verification in the assisted lane

Act

The Assisted Campaign Builder browses the live target page before creating the campaign. If any copy claim in the customer-supplied experience is not supported by the live page, it is flagged for confirmation before activation — never silently applied or removed.

Why it mattersA customer may know something about their product that the live page does not say yet. The VT builds exactly what the customer intended and lets the customer decide — it does not censor without consent.
6

OA tri-state gating

Extract

As of v1.1.0 / v1.2.0 (2026-08-24), both collectors read oa_analytics_configured before attempting any Optimizely Analytics query. false eliminates the query entirely. true produces a specific permissions message on failure. unknown falls back to runtime detection.

Why it mattersA job that attempts an OA query for a customer who said "no" during onboarding produces noise in every source availability matrix. Three-state gating makes source coverage meaningful and failure messages actionable.
7

AGENTS.md as the onboarding state machine

Onboarding

The onboarding imperative and verbatim question text are embedded in AGENTS.md — not only in the onboarding agent's prompt. Any pass that reads AGENTS.md while onboarded: false sees fixed question wording and cannot improvise.

Why it mattersThe Tron Man Tron incident — questions about JavaScript developers, CDPs, and firmographic enrichment — traced directly to the generic IM responder improvising question text. The fix closes that failure mode at the source.
8

Evidence-before-activation philosophy

Enrich

Written into AGENTS.md Operating Rules and enforced throughout: behavioral signals outweigh structural signals; directional lift is not declared a winner; built and staged is not live; activation requires customer approval. The Watchlist flags harmful campaigns, tracking failures, and unsubstantiated copy claims immediately regardless of which job is running.

Why it mattersPersonalization campaigns affect real visitor experiences at scale. A VT that activates prematurely or overstates confidence causes business harm. The philosophy is codified so it cannot be lost in a prompt update.
